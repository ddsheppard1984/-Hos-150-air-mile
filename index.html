§395.1(e)(1) checklist

  • Stay within a 150 air-mile radius (172.6 statute miles) of the normal work reporting location.
  • Report and return to the normal work reporting location and be released within 14 consecutive hours.
  • Have at least 10 consecutive hours off duty separating each 14-hour on-duty period.
  • Do not exceed 11 hours driving.
  • When qualifying, the short-haul exception can allow a time record instead of a RODS/ELD for the day.
Important: The app cannot legally determine that you are exempt. If any qualification fails, standard HOS/RODS requirements may apply. Follow your carrier's safety policy and applicable federal/state rules.

Federal source: FMCSA Hours of Service and Interstate Truck Driver's Guide. The app uses 150 nautical miles = 172.6 statute miles for the geographic test.
