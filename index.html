Prototype / compliance note

This prototype records duty status on the device, provides HOS calculations, a 150-air-mile assistant, GPS tracking, inspections, records and a roadside summary. It does not yet synchronize with the truck ECM and is not a certified FMCSA ELD. Production certification requires compliant hardware integration, required event/data handling, security, transfer, diagnostics and other regulatory requirements.
